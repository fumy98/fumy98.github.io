Why

Stop losing the idea before you reach a laptop

Diagram tools assume a desk. Ideas arrive on the train and in bed. And when you want to compare plan A with plan B, copies of the file pile up and nobody knows which one is current. BoxLine is the notebook that fixes both.

Before

Notes in text, plans scattered across copies

You jot it down in a notes app and redraw it later. Every experiment becomes another duplicate: "architecture_v3_final_2". What changed? Nobody remembers.

BoxLine

Diagram on the spot, plans as branches

Boxes and lines drawn with a finger become nodes and arrows immediately. "Branch from here" creates plan B, the diff is shown in color, and "Adopt" writes the winner back. Every operation stays in the history.

How it works

Three steps to a decision

Draw or speak

Sketch boxes and lines with a finger, or talk to "Diagram from text". They become boxes and arrows.

Branch

Tap the branch chip and choose "Branch from here". Change anything in plan B; main stays untouched.

Compare and adopt

Open "Diff", decide with the colors, and "Adopt" to write it back. Export to draw.io or text when you are done.

FAQ

Questions

Does it run on iPad?

BoxLine is iPhone only. iPad is not supported.

What if a shape is not recognized?

Strokes that are not recognized stay as freehand ink. Right after recognition you can tap "Undo" to turn the box back into ink. Recognition sensitivity has three levels in Settings.

Where does my text go when I use the AI features?

Through the developer's relay server (Cloudflare Workers) to Anthropic's API, used only to generate the diagram. The relay stores nothing except a random device ID generated by the app and the current month's usage count, which expire automatically.

Can AI break my existing diagram?

No. The result is always added as a new branch (only an empty diagram receives it directly in main). You can edit it by hand or delete the branch.

What happens to my data if I cancel Pro?

Your diagrams stay on the device and keep working within the Free limits. "Export the whole notebook" is available on Free too, so you can take your data out as a .boxline file at any time.

What about switching phones?

With Pro iCloud sync, the notebook is simply there on the new phone. Without it, export and import the notebook file. The Pro purchase is restored with "Restore Purchases" in the App Store.

Can I continue a draw.io diagram?

With Pro you can import .drawio and .drawio.svg files. Shapes BoxLine does not have are imported as rectangles. Exported draw.io files open directly in app.diagrams.net.
