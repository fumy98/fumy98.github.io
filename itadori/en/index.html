Features

Only what the shop needs, done right

Several stock lengths at once

Register 2x4 8 ft, 10 ft, 12 ft and more. Add unit prices and Itadori picks the cheapest combination.

Kerf, end trim, usable offcut

Set the kerf per cut, trimming at both ends and the length below which an offcut is scrap. Layouts that leave usable offcuts win.

Plywood sheet layouts

Nest shelves and sides on a 4x8, 4x4 or 5x5 sheet. Guillotine cuts only, so a track saw or panel saw can follow the diagram as is.

Material groups

2x4 and 1x4, pine and oak never get mixed. Parts are only placed on stock from the same group.

Boards, yield and offcuts at a glance

Board count, yield, cost estimate and the offcut list sit at the top of the result. The diagram is color-coded by part.

FAQ

FAQ

Does it run on iPad?

iPhone only. iPad is not supported.

Does it handle fractional inches?

Yes. Type 35 3/4, 35-3/4 or 35.75 and everything is shown to the nearest 1/16 in. You can also switch to mm or cm at any time.

Can it optimize sheet goods (2D)?

Yes. Set the project type to Sheet, enter sheets as long × short side and parts as length × width, and Itadori lays them out with guillotine cuts. Turn off Rotate for parts whose grain must run one way.

How far does the free version go?

The optimizer and the diagram are fully available. Limits are 1 saved project, 2 stock types and 5 part rows.

What happens when I get a new phone?

Export a backup file with Pro and import it on the new phone. The Pro purchase carries over with Restore Purchases.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Nothing is sent to anyone, including the developer.
